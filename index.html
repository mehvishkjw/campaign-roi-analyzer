Cheat sheet

Plain-English glossary

ROI

Profit you earn back for each $1 spent, measured over the customer's first 12 months. 1.0 = break-even. 2.0 = you earn $2 for every $1.

Claimed ROI vs real ROI

Claimed counts every customer a campaign touched last. Real counts only customers the campaign caused who would not have come otherwise.

Incrementality

What share of "credited" customers were truly brought in by the ads. 100% = all of them. 10% = nine in ten would have bought anyway.

Next-dollar return (marginal ROI)

What the next $1 earns, not the average $1. Spending more in a crowded campaign earns less each time, so this decides where extra money should go.

Customer value (12-month profit)

Profit from a customer in their first year after discounts, refunds and repeat orders, predicted by a machine-learning model for newer customers.

How sure are we?

Ranges on each campaign (shown in the panel) come from re-running the model on 300 re-sampled versions of the data. Big moves should still be confirmed with a small test.
